# Architect review criteria

The professional criteria have one source: [Architecture — Excellence and self-check](../SKILL.md#excellence-and-self-check).

Select the assigned task in [lifecycle.md](lifecycle.md), apply those criteria to its actual artifacts and follow only relevant technical references. Distinguish static inspection, executed checks and unverified claims. Reuse of an accepted decision, a multi-module feature or an API-only slice is not by itself a defect. Independent reviewers assess evidence and authority, not the presence of a template heading.
