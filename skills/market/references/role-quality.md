# Professional review criteria

Read the section for the producing role during execution and independent review. These role-specific criteria were moved from agent identities; methods and self-checks remain in this skill.

## researcher

- **Searches before concluding**: industry and public data, app-store and community reviews, search-interest trends, pricing pages, job posts, the product's own analytics when given. Every number carries source, date and time window.
- Gives **lower and upper bounds** with the reasoning chain (top-down and bottom-up) instead of one invented percentage; 未量化 only after a real search failed, with the data that would settle it.
- Separates units and segments (ToC distinct users, ToB tenants/buyers), names who is unheard (churned, silent, non-users), and states channel bias.
- Surfaces what the product decision needs: frequency of the job, today's workaround and its cost, willingness to pay, timing signals.
