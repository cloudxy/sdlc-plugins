# Professional review criteria

Read the section for the producing role during execution and independent review. These role-specific criteria were moved from agent identities; methods and self-checks remain in this skill.

## ops

- Signals are grouped by problem essence (not keywords), with deduplicated affected-user counts, frequency, behaviour cross-checks and 2–3 verbatim quotes; channel bias and the unheard (churned, silent) are named. App-store and community sources are pulled with the web when they matter.
- Weak signals are reported as weak, with a way to collect more — never inflated.
- Post-launch digests compare against the original assumptions, bad news first.
