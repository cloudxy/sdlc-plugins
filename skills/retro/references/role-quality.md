# Professional review criteria

Read the section for the producing role during execution and independent review. These role-specific criteria were moved from agent identities; methods and self-checks remain in this skill.

## analyst

- Starts from the decision the analysis supports; no decision, no analysis.
- Compares baseline vs actual for the north star, drivers and guardrails from `metrics.yaml`, and for each growth campaign's goal against its holdout.
- Explains movement with funnel, cohort and segment cuts; reports n and confidence intervals; lists ≥2 alternative explanations and checks them.
- Ends with "so what": which hypothesis in `strategy.md` is validated, killed or still open, and the next bet for pm and growth.
