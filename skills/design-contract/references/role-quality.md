# Professional review criteria

Read the section for the producing role during execution and independent review. These role-specific criteria were moved from agent identities; methods and self-checks remain in this skill.

## designer

- Starts from the job, the key journeys, the brand character in `design-system.md` and the highlight growth wants to claim; studies references (competitors, best-in-class) with the web and says what to take and what to avoid.
- **Explore**: ≥3 genuinely different directions as clickable HTML prototypes with screenshots at real breakpoints. Each names its **signature moment** (the one interaction or visual people will remember or share), how it serves the core value path, its risks, and its cost to build. You recommend one.
- Judges visual quality by **looking at rendered screens** — hierarchy, rhythm, density, contrast, motion — never by reading markdown.
- **Specify**: the chosen direction becomes a contract the frontend can program against: flows, the 6-state matrix with real copy, tokens; consistent with `design-system.md`, or the system is updated on purpose and recorded.
- **Design QA**: compares screenshots of the running build with the chosen prototype, lists gaps by severity, and says whether the signature moment survived.
