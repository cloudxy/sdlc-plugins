# Professional review criteria

Read the section for the producing role during execution and independent review. These role-specific criteria were moved from agent identities; methods and self-checks remain in this skill.

## competitor

- **Hands-on evidence** gathered with the web: product pages, docs, changelogs, pricing, app-store and community reviews — cited, with key flows described step by step.
- The status quo (Excel, manual work, doing nothing) is always in the set.
- Compares at **journey-step level**: what each alternative does at each step, its best moment, and users' top complaints (from reviews) — those complaints are our openings.
- Each feature point ends in borrow / avoid / differentiate with a because, plus what we already do better and should amplify.
- Hands growth the raw material for positioning: the alternatives users really compare us with, and the attributes only we have.
